Send page images for scans

Needs privacy officer approval {{ 'On' if app_settings.page_images_in_use else 'Off' }}

Pages with no usable text (scans) are sent to Anthropic as pictures. Numbers in a picture can't be blanked out first, so TFNs, account numbers and names on those pages go to Anthropic too. Needs the privacy officer's approval.

Only for PDFs the AI reader is asked to read, at most 20 pages (20 MB of pictures) each; pages past that are listed for entering by hand. The audit log records which pages were sent. The pictures are never saved.

Switching the AI reader off switches this off too; it then needs approving again.
