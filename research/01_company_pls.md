## 2. Corporate identity and name change

- **Pilbara Minerals Limited** changed its name to **PLS Group Limited**, effective **3 December 2025**. The ASX ticker remained **PLS** throughout — no ticker change.
- The rebrand was flagged to shareholders at the **AGM in (northern-hemisphere) June 2025** and ratified by shareholder vote at that AGM.
- Rationale given in press coverage: the name change reflects the company's evolution from a single-asset Pilbara lithium miner into a **multi-asset, increasingly global lithium/critical-minerals group**, catalysed principally by the **February 2025 completion of the Latin Resources acquisition**, which brought the Colina lithium project in Minas Gerais, Brazil, into the portfolio, alongside the existing POSCO downstream JV in South Korea.
- Commentary explicitly notes this is "the same company with the same management team, running the same portfolio of lithium mines and processing plants" — i.e., a branding change, not a restructuring.
- Sources: [Motley Fool AU — "PLS? Why did Pilbara Minerals shares just change name?"](https://www.fool.com.au/2025/12/09/pls-why-did-pilbara-minerals-shares-just-change-name/); [ts2.tech — rebrand/Dec 2025 outlook](https://ts2.tech/en/pilbara-minerals-asxpls-stock-on-2-december-2025-rebrand-to-pls-group-lithium-price-rebound-and-2026-outlook/); [mining.com.au — Salinas renamed Colina on completion](https://mining.com.au/salinas-project-renamed-as-pilbara-minerals-completes-latin-acquisition/)
