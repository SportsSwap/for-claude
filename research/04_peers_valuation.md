## ⚠️ METHODOLOGY & DATA-QUALITY CAVEATS — READ FIRST

1. **WebFetch was unavailable for this entire research task.** Every domain attempted (stockanalysis.com, marketindex.com.au, simplywall.st, finance.yahoo.com, asx.com.au, reuters.com, bloomberg.com, sec.gov, en.wikipedia.org, pilbaraminerals.com.au, fnarena.com, stockhead.com.au, google.com) returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` from the sandbox's network proxy. All data below comes from **WebSearch result synthesis** (Claude's search tool reading multiple snippets and summarizing), which cites real source URLs but could not be cross-verified by opening the primary pages directly.
2. **WebSearch hit its session budget cap (200/200) before Section 4 (broker-by-broker PLS targets) and Section 5 (WACC inputs) could be fully completed.** These gaps are marked **NOT FOUND** explicitly rather than estimated. If you need these figures, the session budget needs to be raised or the queries re-run in a fresh session.
3. **Different data providers returned materially different figures for the same metric on the same day** (e.g., BHP market cap, several EV/EBITDA readings). Rather than silently picking one, both/all readings are shown with their source so you can judge which vintage/methodology to use.
4. **Currency**: BHP and Rio Tinto report underlying financials in **US$** (dual-listed, ASX price/market cap in A$). Fortescue and South32 also report underlying financials in **US$** but pay dividends in mixed currencies. Mineral Resources and PLS report in **A$**. This is preserved per line item below — do not casually cross-add USD and AUD figures.
5. **No multiple, price target, or deal metric below has been invented.** Where a figure could not be found or reliably derived, it is marked **NOT FOUND**. Two EV/resource figures in Section 3 are explicitly labelled **"derived by us"** (simple division of two independently-sourced, cited figures) rather than a broker-published multiple — flagged as such throughout.
