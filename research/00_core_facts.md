**Bearish forces right now (must be addressed honestly):**
- Mine restarts adding supply: **MinRes Bald Hill** (idled since Nov-2024, restarted, first shipment targeted Q1 FY27),
  **CATL Jianxiawo**, **Core Lithium Finniss**.
- One source projects global lithium output growth **>25% p.a. through 2029**.
- Prices have tested a **three-month low**; spodumene fell sharply w/w in Aug-26.

**Bullish forces:**
- China power + energy-storage battery production **191.7 GWh in May-2026, +55.2% YoY**.
- BESS / AI-datacentre-driven power demand is a genuine new demand leg.
- Reported **SC6 structural deficit of ~120 kt LCE in 2026** (source: ESI — NEEDS CORROBORATION).
- BMI revised lithium price forecasts **upwards** on supply tightening (Discovery Alert — NEEDS CORROBORATION).
- PLS CEO on the record: spodumene swing supply "should act rationally" amid the price uptrend (Fastmarkets).

> **Thesis implication:** the honest framing is NOT "prices go up in a straight line". It is that PLS is a
> **low-cost, long-life, balance-sheet-fortified swing-supply owner** that earns money through the cycle and
> holds the option on the upswing. That is a far more defensible pitch than a price-forecast bet.
