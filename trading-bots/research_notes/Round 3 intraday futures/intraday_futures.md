# Intraday / short-swing rules on NQ/ES/QQQ/SPY: research notes (Round 3)
Compiled 2026-11-27. Every number carries its source. Primary PDFs were downloaded and read in full (marked [PDF]); blogs/GitHub are low weight.

## 1. Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon 2024/25 "Beat the Market" (Noise Area), SPY
Source [PDF]: https://alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/a99aba00-f967-49b3-aceb-f544dc386e0b/content (version 3 Feb 2025; SFI 24-97)
Data: SPY 1-min IQFeed, May 2007-Apr 2024, 2,620 test days (Table 5).
Rules (exact):
- move(t-i, HH:MM) = |Close(t-i,HH:MM)/Open(t-i,9:30) - 1|, i=1..14. sigma(t,HH:MM) = mean of those 14 values (same minute-of-day).
- UpperBound = max(Open_t, Close_{t-1,16:00}) * (1 + VM*sigma); LowerBound = min(Open_t, Close_{t-1}) * (1 - VM*sigma). VM=1 in base (Fig 9: best Sharpe near VM~1.5 but they keep 1).
- Trade only at HH:00 and HH:30 (first trade 10:00, per Sec 4.3). Price above Upper -> long; below Lower -> short. Stops also evaluated only on the half-hour.
- Exit: close 16:00, or stop. Base stop = opposite band (reverse position on crossover). Final stop: long exits if price < max(UpperBound, VWAP); short exits if price > min(LowerBound, VWAP). VWAP computed on RTH data only. After a stop, a new entry is possible later (7,668 trades in 17y ~ 1.8 per traded day).
- Sizing: shares = AUM_{t-1} * min(4, 0.02/sigma_SPY) / Open_t, where sigma_SPY = stdev of last 14 daily returns (daily vol target 2%, leverage cap 4x).
- Costs: $0.0035/share commission (IB entry; $0.002 above 300k sh/mo) + $0.001/share slippage (measured from 1,000+ live market orders, April 2024, ~$100k each).
Results (net): opposite-band stop, 100% size: IRR 6.2%, vol 10.9%, Sharpe 0.61, MDD 21% (Table 1). Curr.Band+VWAP, 100% size: IRR 9.7%, vol 7.7%, Sharpe 1.24, MDD 12%, hit ratio 43%. + vol targeting: total return 1,985%, IRR 19.6%, vol 14.3%, Sharpe 1.33, MDD 25%, alpha 19.6%, beta -0.07 (Table 3). SPY B&H: 7.2% IRR, Sharpe 0.45, MDD 56%.
Conditioning: Sharpe rises with VIX (>40: ~3.5). Day-of-week avg PnL bps: Mon 9, Tue 9, Wed 18 (t 3.42), Thu 12, Fri 13; unconditional 12 bps t 5.34. After NR4 day 22 bps (t 5.14, n=660), NR7 16 bps (t 3.07, n=367), after trend day -2 bps (Table 5, 6). Lower prior-day RSI(5) -> higher profit (beta -3.25, p=0.001).
Reliability: authors run a trading-education/research business (Concretum, Bear Bull Traders); costs are modest; Matlab, no OOS. Vol targeting nearly doubles the return (Sharpe 1.24->1.33 only), so the headline 19.6% is mostly leverage. Only SPY tested, not futures.
Independent checks (snippets only; GitHub fetch blocked in this session, so not read in full):
- SSRN 7609644 "Noise-band Intraday Momentum in Nasdaq-100 Futures" (Justin Chuk) per search snippet: +$10.26 per trading day net, daily Sharpe 1.14, and 1 index point of slippage on stop fills removes 22% of the edge. URL: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7609644 (could not open; snippet from search result).
- GitHub replication list incl. VigorTas/beat-the-market-replication ("16 years of Nasdaq and S&P 500 futures, after costs"), PazSheimy/spy-intraday-momentum-oos, giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy: found via search, unreadable here. One reported train Sharpe 0.72-1.07 across lookback 7-90d and multipliers 0.8-1.5, i.e. no stable optimum (search snippet, unverified).
- arXiv 2605.04004 (Mesfin, MNQ 2021-2025, 947 days, 5-min): 14 OHLCV intraday momentum signal families, none passes OOS net-of-friction test (2.0 pt friction floor); ORB long T=0.88; gap continuation short gross +16.5 pts but T=1.46. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004

## 2. Zarattini-Aziz ORB on QQQ/TQQQ (2023) and Zarattini-Barbon-Aziz "stocks in play" (2024)
### 2a. QQQ/TQQQ paper [PDF]: https://concretumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Can-Day-Trading-Really-Be-Profitable.pdf (SSRN 4416622)
Data: IB aggregated data, 1 Jan 2016 - 17 Feb 2023. $25k start, commission $0.0005/share, NO slippage.
Rules: first 5-min candle (9:30-9:35) direction. Bullish -> long at open of second candle (9:35 open); bearish -> short; doji -> no trade. Stop = low of first candle (long) / high (short); R = |entry - stop|. Target 10R else exit at close (EoD). Size: shares = int(min(A*0.01/R, 4*A/P)), P = open of 2nd candle (1% risk per trade, 4x leverage cap).
Results: QQQ ORB total return 676%, yearly 33%, vol 29%, Sharpe 1.13, MDD 22%; 1,795 trades, 51% long, win rate 24%, avg +0.13R, alpha 33%. TQQQ ORB 1,484%, 48%/yr, vol 39%, Sharpe 1.19, MDD 28%, avg +0.18R. Buy&hold QQQ 169%/MDD 36%, TQQQ 438%/MDD 82%. Variant (TQQQ, stop 5% of 14-day ATR, EoD exit) 9,350% (authors flag unrealistic: stop $0.08 wide, no slippage).
Reliability: no slippage, no OOS; stop is the 1st-candle extreme (on NQ ~ 20-30 points).
### 2b. Independent costed replication (blog, moderate-low weight): https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235 (Sep 2026)
Same rule on NQ/SPX/Dow/DAX/FTSE CFDs, 2015-Jun 2026. NQ gross +0.131R (t 2.9, win 23.2%, 10R hits 2.4%) reproduces paper. After spread+slippage (NQ 2.5 pts, SPX 0.8) NQ net +0.002R (t 0.0), SPX -0.081R. Rule beats random-direction bracket by +0.124R on NQ (t 2.1): the first-candle colour has about 0.1R information. 2015-2017 negative in every market; 2021-2026 OOS NQ at best +0.06R.
### 2c. Tradingedgejournal ES/NQ 2010-2026 (blog): https://tradingedgejournal.com/blog/opening-range-breakout-futures-backtest/
384 breakout variants (5/15/30/60 min ranges) + 96 failed-breakout variants on ES: 0 survivors, all negative in confirm months. Costs 0.615 pt ES, 0.788 pt NQ round trip. A published 5-min ORB (solid candle, 1.4 ATR stop, 2R): ES -0.02R (2025-26), -0.23R (2010-24); NQ -0.04R, -0.06R. ES 5m ORB lost -0.22 to -0.45R/trade every year 2010-2020. 5-min close beyond first-hour range returns inside within 60 min 67% ES / 65% NQ.
### 2d. Stocks-in-play paper [PDF]: https://www.alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/3c2989c4-688d-4d78-8a71-f02690990d51/content (SSRN 4729284)
US stocks 2016-2023 (not index products). Filters: open > $5, 14d avg volume >= 1,000,000, 14d ATR > $0.50, Relative Volume (first-5-min volume / mean of prior 14 days' first-5-min volume) >= 100%, top 20 by RelVol each day. Direction by first 5-min candle colour, stop-order at OR high/low, stop 10% of 14d ATR from entry, exit 16:00, risk 1%/trade, max leverage 4x, $0.0035/share. Base (no RelVol): IRR 3.2%, Sharpe 0.48. +RelVol: total 1,637%, IRR 41.6%, vol 14.8%, Sharpe 2.81, MDD 12%. 15-min ORB Sharpe 1.43, 30-min 0.21, 60-min 0.40. Not applicable to NQ/ES directly (no relative-volume cross-section).

## 3. Gao-Han-Li-Zhou (JFE 2018) and Baltussen-Da-Lammers-Martens (JFE 2021)
Gao et al: SPY 1993-2013. r1 = return from previous close to 10:00 (first half-hour incl. overnight); r12 = 15:00-15:30; target r13 = last half-hour 15:30-16:00. In-sample R2 ~1.6% (r1 alone); first+12th combined 2.6%; OOS ~1.2% (summary via search: https://papers.ssrn.com/ ; https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:129:y:2018:i:2:p:394-414 ; R2 1.6% confirmed in https://temperancecapital.substack.com/p/market-intraday-momentum-replicates). Original paper PDF not fetched.
Baltussen et al [PDF] https://www3.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf (JFE 142, 2021): 60+ futures 1974-2020. Definitions: day = close t-1 to close t; ONFH = overnight + first half hour; ROD (rest of day) = close(t-1) to 15:30; rule eta = +r_LH if signal>0 else -r_LH (long if r>0 else short for last 30 min, 15:30-16:00). Equity index futures (Panel A, Table 6, gross, no cost): signal rONFH avg 4.21%/yr, vol 3.95, Sharpe 1.07, success 0.55; rROD 6.86%/yr, Sharpe 1.73; both-agree 5.47%, Sharpe 1.60; always-long LH Sharpe 0.11. Text: in S&P E-mini the net Sharpe stays positive if cost = 1 tick. Mechanism: short-gamma hedging near close; effect stronger on high-vol/ high-gamma days.
Post-2013 / replications (blogs):
- https://temperancecapital.substack.com/p/market-intraday-momentum-replicates : SPY 2000-2019 slope 0.060 (t 3.30, R2 1.39%); 2000-07 0.053 (t 3.31), 2008-12 0.091 (t 2.64), 2013-19 0.003 (t 0.20); mean |last-half-hour move| 20.1 bps.
- https://deadsignalslab.substack.com/p/replication-intraday-momentum-eight : SPY/QQQ/IWM/DIA, 2-bp round trip, 2014-2026 -2.30 bps/day net (t -5.17); SPY and DIA show slight reversal; only IWM has gross momentum (+1.4 bp, pre-2018).
Conclusion: real in-sample 1993-2012, large in 2008; ~0 since 2013 on SPY; not tradable net of costs except in futures with ~1 tick cost and high-vol regimes.

## 4. Overnight drift (Boyarchenko-Larsen-Whelan, RFS 2023) [PDF]: https://research-api.cbs.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/101311522/nina_boyarchenko_et_al_the_overnight_drift_acceptedversion.pdf
ES futures 1998-2020. 02:00-03:00 ET (European open) averages 3.7% annualised (1.48 bps/day); DST shifts it to 04:00-05:00 in the 3 weeks US/Europe are out of sync. Strategies 2004-2020 (spread cost from quotes): long 02:00-03:00 Sharpe 1.1 gross, -0.5 net of bid-ask; long 01:30-03:30 gross Sharpe 1.3, net 0.3; "buy the dip" (OD+ only after prior-day closing relative signed volume < 0, ~50% of days) Sharpe 1.8 gross, 1.1 net. Return window 3.8%/yr OD (CTO 4.6%, OTC 4.2%, close-to-close 8.9%, Sharpe 0.42). Mechanism: dealer inventory from closing imbalance.
Post-publication (NY Fed, 1 Jul 2026): https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/ : 2:00-3:00 window earned ~3.7%/yr before; Jan 2021-Dec 2025 ES, NQ and YM are all flat (~0). Std of closing-hour order imbalance fell from 6.5% to 2.9%. NightShares overnight ETFs closed after 14 months. => Not tradable after costs and apparently gone since 2021. Only an imbalance-conditioned version had a net edge pre-2021 (needs signed volume data, not in OHLC).

## 5. Gaps, Turnaround Tuesday
- Gap fill timing (descriptive, blog) https://tradingstats.net/when-do-gaps-fill/ : ES 803 / NQ 839 days Jan 2020-Apr 2025 in which the gap was eventually filled (conditional on fill!): 25.5% ES / 34.0% NQ filled in first 5 min; 51.4% / 60.9% by 30 min; 74.2% / 78.7% by 90 min. Not a tradable expectancy (selects filled days).
- Other fill-rate claims (59-60% ES, 57-59% NQ over six months; small gaps 84% fill) are vendor material without methodology: https://www.edgeful.com/blog/posts/5-minute-opening-range-breakout-es-strategy , https://nexusfi.com/a/concepts/fill-the-gap . Do not trust.
- MNQ gap continuation short gross +16.5 pts but not significant (T 1.46) in arXiv 2605.04004 above.
- Academic: Lou-Polk-Skouras (overnight vs intraday opposite sign, stock-level); LSE 2026 paper reports market-level intraday mean reversion to past overnight returns (search snippet; https://personal.lse.ac.uk/POLK/research/LPS_ClientelePerspectiveOnEquityPremium_20260603.pdf not read). Yu-Rentzler-Wolf 2005 JOIM on NQ: momentum vs reversal depends on signs of yesterday/overnight returns and Monday (https://www.joim.com/nasdaq-100-index-futures-intraday-momentum-reversal, not read).
- Turnaround Tuesday: rule = buy Monday close if Monday close < Friday close, sell Tuesday close, max 1 trade/week. ES daily 11/1997-4/2026 1 contract: avg trade $187.67, profit factor 1.48, final equity $109,037.75; return/maxDD 7.39 (https://algotr.substack.com/p/turnaround-tuesday-the-one-day-edge, paywalled, the author's filtered version claims PF 2.26). SPY version (Monday down >=1%): 212 trades, avg +0.3%, win 56%, CAGR 1.8%, exposure 2.5% (https://quantifiedstrategies.substack.com/p/turnaround-tuesday-strategy-backtest). Schaeffer's: since 2010 after Monday <= -1.5% Tuesday avg +0.9%, ~70% up (https://schaeffersresearch.com/content/analysis/2021/09/22/is-turnaround-thursday-a-real-thing). Small samples; edge is low-frequency mean reversion, which is consistent with the Zarattini NR4/low RSI finding.

## 6. ICT / smart money
- https://www.backtestingmax.com/backtests/ict-silver-bullet : ICT Silver Bullet (sweep of recent high/low then FVG entry in 10-11 NY window, etc.), 2020-2025, 1% risk, costs incl. 10 tests: all lost. MNQ 1-min -32.1% (1,323 trades, win 25.4%, PF 0.96; gross slightly positive but costs $33,397 > edge); MNQ 5-min -26.2% (206 trades, PF 0.74); MES 1-min -56% (PF 0.91), MES 5-min -18.1% (PF 0.82). 0 of 10 profitable in 2020-23.
- Nordman Algorithms (38,114 FVG retests, ES/NQ/6E/6A, Oct 2023-Sep 2026): price returns to 73.0% of real FVGs vs 72.9% of random equal-distance levels, so "gaps must fill" is false; real FVGs outperform random levels by +3.7% (5 min) and +2.5% (15 min) edge ratio, gone by 1h; edge is 1-3 ticks in ES/NQ, below trade cost. https://www.nordman-algorithms.com/blog/do-fair-value-gaps-work/
- A dev.to post (2,391 NQ trades 2014-2026, PF 0.77) was cited by search but URL returned 404; treat as unverified.
Verdict: no credible evidence of a net edge; all are vendor/blog level, no peer review.

## 7. Crabel ORB / NR7 / Williams breakout
- Crabel ORB (open +/- fraction of prior ranges) is cited in Zarattini papers but not tested there. Zarattini noise paper lists Crabel/Kaufman volatility breakouts as predecessors (PDF above).
- NR7 / NR4 as a CONDITION (best independent-ish evidence): Zarattini Table 5, SPY, avg PnL of the noise-area strategy on the day after NR4 22 bps vs 12 bps unconditional.
- Oxford Strat NR7 on 42 futures, 1980-2016: "better when NR_Length >= 6 but not tradeable after costs without additional rules" (https://oxfordstrat.com/trading-strategies/narrow-range/ , numbers not read). Quantified Strategies says S&P ORB no longer works (https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/?p=119785).
- Larry Williams volatility breakout (entry = open + k*prior-day range, k typically 0.5-0.6; long entry; exit at next open or close): I found NO independent costed test on ES/NQ (only indicator pages, https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/volatility-breakout/). Treat as untested; backtest k in {0.3..1.0} on your own data.

## 8. VWAP trend (Zarattini-Aziz 2023) [PDF]: https://concretumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Volume-Weighted-Average-Price.pdf
QQQ & TQQQ, 1-min, 2 Jan 2018 - 28 Sep 2023, $25k, $0.0005/sh, NO slippage, no leverage (100% equity every trade).
Rules: at 9:31 (after first 1-min bar closes): close > VWAP (RTH-only) -> long, else short. Exit and reverse when a 1-min candle CLOSES on the other side of VWAP (a wick through is ignored). Always in the market until 16:00 close. ~21,967 trades in 5.75 years (about 15 per day!).
Results: QQQ total 671%, 43%/yr, vol 18%, Sharpe 2.1, MDD 9.4%, hit ratio ~17%, gain:loss ratio ~5.5; B&H QQQ 126%, Sharpe 0.7, MDD 35.6%. TQQQ 8,242%, 116%/yr, Sharpe 1.7, MDD 36.1% (22,399 trades, commissions $400k). Commissions QQQ $6,547 total.
Reliability: HIGH risk of cost overstatement: 0 slippage and 15 trades/day with 17% win rate; QQQ 1-min spread 1 cent ~ 2 bps x 15 trades = large. Authors say unsuitable for multimillion accounts and note commissions matter. A QuantConnect reproduction reports CAGR 40.1%, Sharpe 1.75, fees $6.5k (https://www.quantconnect.com/terminal/cache/embedded_backtest_b115d0894231d55994b1068202f6c0ae.html, search snippet). A QuantConnect forum thread "not so fast" (https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/16706/) challenged it; content not readable. I did not find a costed NQ futures test.

## Cross-cutting conclusions
1. Every Zarattini result uses zero or tiny slippage; independent costed tests (mql5 blog, tradingedgejournal, arXiv 2605.04004) put ORB-type breakouts at ~0 net on NQ/ES. Cost in points: ES 0.6, NQ 0.8 round trip for minis (tradingedgejournal); MNQ/MES micro commission $0.62/side per backtestingmax.
2. The edge that persists across sources: (a) direction of the first 5-30 min (first candle) has ~0.1R information on NQ; (b) vol-regime conditioning (high VIX, low prior RSI, after NR4); (c) low-frequency mean reversion after down days (Turnaround Tuesday).
3. Decayed/dead: Gao last-half-hour momentum on SPY since 2013, overnight drift since 2021.
